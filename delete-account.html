Account Deletion Instructions

If you would like to delete your account and associated data from the Kshatriya app, please follow the steps below:

Method 1: Delete from within the App

  1. Open the Kshatriya app on your device.
  2. Go to your Profile or Settings menu.
  3. Select the Account option.
  4. Tap on Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice when prompted. Your account and personal data will be permanently deleted.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us:

  • Email us at: support@kshatriya.org.in (or the relevant support email)
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Include the email address or phone number associated with your account in the email body.

We will process your request and delete your account and associated data within 14 days.
